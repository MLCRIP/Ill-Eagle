# Contributing to Ill Eagle

Ill Eagle is an independent, actively developed modification of HeliBoard maintained by MLCRIP. It does not currently claim an established contributor community or guarantee that proposed changes will be accepted.

## Issues

Use the [Ill Eagle issue tracker](https://github.com/MLCRIP/Ill-Eagle/issues) for Ill Eagle bugs and feature proposals. Search existing issues first and include enough detail to reproduce a bug. Questions about unmodified HeliBoard behaviour may be better answered by the [upstream HeliBoard project](https://github.com/HeliBorg/HeliBoard), but do not report an Ill Eagle-specific problem as an upstream HeliBoard defect.

Dictionary-source questions can refer to the [upstream dictionary collection](https://codeberg.org/Helium314/aosp-dictionaries). Ill Eagle's current dictionary compliance status is documented in [docs/DICTIONARY_LICENSING.md](docs/DICTIONARY_LICENSING.md).

## Proposed changes

Before proposing a substantial change, open an Ill Eagle issue describing the problem and intended result. A proposal should:

- preserve unrelated working behaviour;
- avoid changing package identity, signing, or distribution configuration without prior discussion;
- include proportionate tests for behaviour changes;
- identify copied or adapted third-party material and its source and licence;
- remain compatible with GPL-3.0-only distribution of the combined program; and
- avoid adding dependencies or assets whose redistribution terms have not been established.

Submission does not guarantee review, acceptance, or a release schedule.

## AI-assisted work

Ill Eagle has not adopted HeliBoard's upstream AI contribution policy as an Ill Eagle licence condition. Regardless of the tools used, a person proposing a change remains responsible for understanding it, testing it, and establishing that included material can be distributed under the project's applicable licences. See [AI_USAGE.md](AI_USAGE.md) for the preserved upstream-policy context.

## Upstream policies

The original HeliBoard contribution rules apply to contributions submitted to HeliBoard, not automatically to independent Ill Eagle development. Consult the current [HeliBoard contribution guide](https://github.com/HeliBorg/HeliBoard/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [HeliBoard AI policy](https://github.com/HeliBorg/HeliBoard/blob/main/AI_USAGE.md) before interacting with that upstream project.

## Licensing

Ill Eagle is distributed as a whole under GPL-3.0-only, while incorporated third-party components retain their applicable notices and licence terms. By proposing original changes for inclusion, ensure that you have the right to provide them on terms compatible with the repository's licensing. Do not remove existing copyright, attribution, SPDX, modification, or warranty notices.
